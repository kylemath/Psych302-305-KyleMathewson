Week 8 · project plan and chats

Start your project

Tonight you hand in your project plan, have a 5-minute chat about it with the instructor, and make the first real file for your project. No analysis, no results page: just start making it.

1 · The plan

Write your plan

Answer the questions in week10-project/CHECKIN.md and save it to GitHub. Then paste it into the midterm box on Canvas. Due tonight at 21:00. Details and marks: project page.

2 · The chat

Talk it over

The instructor meets with each of you for about 5 minutes. Bring your plan, or just your ideas. You leave with a first step you both agree on. The chat is worth 3 of the 15 midterm points.

3 · Start making it

Make your first file

While you wait for your chat, or right after it, make the first real file for your project and put it in week10-project/. Anything that gets you started counts:

  • a web page with a title and one button or picture;
  • a short script that loads some data and prints something;
  • a sketch of a game or quiz screen;
  • a data file you found, with a note on where it came from;
  • a notes file with links and a rough to-do list.

Small is fine. Save it to GitHub before you leave.
