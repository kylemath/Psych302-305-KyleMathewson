What can my project be?

Anything that uses code and has something to do with psychology or how people think and act. For example: test something on yourself, look at data you already have or data shared online, make a game, a quiz, a demo, a tool, or a graph that explains something. You can build on something from class or try something new. Big or small is up to you.

What you do not need

A finished project, a result that matches your guess, a partner, or fancy statistics. Use yourself as the participant. If you want to collect data from classmates, ask the instructor first.

Midterm · 15 points = 15% of your grade

Your project plan

Due: Wednesday 28 October, 21:00 (in class).
What to do: open week10-project/CHECKIN.md in your course repository, answer the questions in it (about one page is enough), and save it to GitHub.
Then paste into Canvas: (1) week10-project/CHECKIN.md, (2) one sentence with your idea and your first step, and (3) whether you used an AI tool, and if so what for.

How it is marked

4 points · Your idea
What you want to make or find out, and why it interests you.
4 points · How you will do it
What you will build or do, what you will use, and any data. If you have a guess about what you will find, write it down now.
4 points · Your steps
A first step (before 18 November), what you hope to have by 2 December, and a bonus goal if things go well.
3 points · What you have and what might go wrong
What you can start from, one thing that might go wrong, and what you will do if it does.
Required, no points · Use of AI
Did you use an AI tool? If yes, what did you ask, keep, and change?

We mark how clear your plan is, not how big it is. Your plan can change later. Missed class on 28 October for a reason the instructor accepts? Hand it in by Friday 30 October, 17:00.

Final · 35 points = 35% of your grade

Your progress report and talk

Due: Wednesday 2 December, 21:00 (last class).
What to do: save all your project files (code, web pages, data, pictures, notes) in week10-project/, answer the questions in week10-project/FINAL.md, and save it all to GitHub.
Then paste into Canvas: (1) week10-project/FINAL.md, (2) one sentence on what you planned and where it is now, and (3) whether you used an AI tool, and if so what for.

Your work and report 30 points + Talk 5 points = 35

Your work and FINAL.md · 30 points

10 points · The work you did
Your project files are saved in week10-project/. They do not have to be finished or working.
7 points · What you did
What you planned, what you actually did, and where the project is now.
6 points · One thing to show
A picture, graph, screenshot, result, or something that works, with a sentence on what it shows.
5 points · What changed and what is next
What changed from your plan and why, what did not work, and what you would do next.
2 points · Use of AI
Did you use an AI tool? If yes, what did you ask, keep, and change?

Talk · 5 points

On Wednesday 2 December, take 3 minutes in front of the class. Say what you planned, show what you made, share one thing you learned, and say what you would do next. No slides needed. Everyone gives a talk.

Missed the talk for a reason the instructor accepts? Give it by Friday 4 December, 17:00. If you hand in neither the report nor the talk, the Faculty rules for a missed final apply.

You do not lose marks for: an unfinished project, a small project, a guess that turned out wrong, or a plain-looking page.
