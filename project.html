What can my project be?

Anything that uses code and has something to do with psychology or how people think and act. For example: test something on yourself, look at data you already have or data shared online, make a game, a quiz, a demo, a tool, or a graph that explains something. You can build on something from class or try something new. Big or small is up to you.

What you do not need

A finished project, a result that matches your guess, a partner, or fancy statistics. Use yourself as the participant. If you want to collect data from classmates, ask the instructor first.

Midterm · 15 points = 15% of your grade

Your project plan and a chat with the instructor

Due: Wednesday 28 October, 21:00 (in class).
Step 1 · Write your plan: open week10-project/CHECKIN.md in your course repository, answer the questions in it (about one page is enough), and save it to GitHub.
Step 2 · Paste into Canvas: (1) week10-project/CHECKIN.md, (2) one sentence with your idea and your first step, and (3) whether you used an AI tool, and if so what for.
Step 3 · Talk it over: during class the instructor meets with each student for about 5 minutes. Bring your plan. You talk through your idea together and agree on a first step.

Written plan 12 points + Chat 3 points = 15

How it is marked

3 points · Your idea
Full marks: what you want to make or find out, and why, clear enough that someone else could explain it back. Some marks: the idea is vague or hard to follow.
3 points · How you will do it
Full marks: what you will build or do, and what you will use (and any data). Some marks: only a general idea of how.
3 points · Your steps
Full marks: a first step, what you hope to have by 2 December, and a bonus goal. Some marks: one or two of these.
3 points · What you have and what might go wrong
Full marks: what you can start from, one thing that might go wrong, and a backup plan. Some marks: only part of this.
3 points · Chat with the instructor
Full marks: you come to the 5-minute chat with your plan and leave with a first step you both agree on. No marks: no chat.
Required, no points · Use of AI
Did you use an AI tool? If yes, what did you ask, keep, and change?

A missing part gets no marks. We mark how clear your plan is, not how big it is, and your plan can change later. Missed class on 28 October for a reason the instructor accepts? Hand in the plan by Friday 30 October, 17:00, and email the instructor to set up your chat.

Final · 35 points = 35% of your grade

Your progress report and a talk to the class

Due: Wednesday 2 December, 21:00 (last class).
Step 1 · Save your work: put all your project files (code, web pages, data, pictures, notes) in week10-project/, answer the questions in week10-project/FINAL.md, and save it all to GitHub.
Step 2 · Paste into Canvas: (1) week10-project/FINAL.md, (2) one sentence on what you planned and where it is now, and (3) whether you used an AI tool, and if so what for.
Step 3 · Present: in the last class, give a 3-minute talk to the class about your project.

Your work and report 30 points + Talk 5 points = 35

How it is marked

10 points · The work you did
Full marks: your files are in week10-project/ and show real work since your plan. They do not have to be finished or working. Some marks: only a little work, or files that are hard to find.
7 points · What you did
Full marks: FINAL.md clearly says what you planned, what you actually did, and where the project is now. Some marks: one of these is missing or unclear.
6 points · One thing to show
Full marks: a picture, graph, screenshot, result, or something that works, plus a sentence on what it shows. Some marks: something to see with no explanation, or the other way round.
5 points · What changed and what is next
Full marks: honest about what changed from your plan and why, and a clear next step. Some marks: only one of these.
2 points · Use of AI
Full marks: you say whether you used an AI tool, and if so what you asked, kept, and changed.
5 points · Talk to the class
Full marks: about 3 minutes in front of the class, covering what you planned, what you made (show it), one thing you learned, and what you would do next. Some marks: some of these are missing. No slides needed. Everyone gives a talk.

A missing part gets no marks. Missed the talk for a reason the instructor accepts? Give it by Friday 4 December, 17:00. If you hand in neither the report nor the talk, the Faculty rules for a missed final apply.

You do not lose marks for: an unfinished project, a small project, a guess that turned out wrong, or a plain-looking page.

For both parts

Rules for the midterm and the final

Work on your own. Save your files to GitHub: a file that is only on your laptop does not count as handed in. Do not include classmates’ names or their data. Late work and letter grades follow the Simple Syllabus on Canvas.
