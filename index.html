Faculty of Science + Faculty of Arts

How the mind computes, and how we make that visible.

A 300-level lab studio for one 3-hour session each week. Science students learn to measure a mental process. Arts students learn to stage that process as an experience. Everyone leaves able to run, modify, and explain one equation that has organized attention research for fifty years.

RT = a + bN Reaction time grows with set size N. The slope b is the computation: milliseconds the mind spends on each extra item.

Why this computation

Slope is a theory you can see

Feature Integration Theory says color can pop out in parallel, while a color-and-shape binding is assembled item by item. That claim becomes a number: feature search slopes near 0 ms/item; conjunction search does not.

Arts half of the room

Composition is a variable

The search display is a field of marks. Palette, grouping, and figure-ground are not decoration. They change what the visual system binds, so aesthetic choices are experimental factors.

Science half of the room

A linear model is enough

At 300-level we do not need mixed-effects theater. Students collect their own RTs, compute two slopes, and argue whether their mind behaved like the textbook figure.

Anatomy of the 3-hour block

Same ritual every week, so mixed majors can breathe

  1. 0:00–0:25 · Frame. One phenomenon, one equation, one prediction on the board.
  2. 0:25–0:40 · Demo. Instructor runs the page once. Students call the prediction.
  3. 0:40–1:15 · First make. Students become subjects. Data appear live.
  4. 1:15–1:25 · Break. Standing, water, no new content.
  1. 1:25–2:05 · Compute. Means, slope, a sentence of interpretation.
  2. 2:05–2:45 · Remix. Arts fork: restyle the display. Science fork: add a factor.
  3. 2:45–3:00 · Critique. One screen, one number, one doubt. Then done.
  4. After class. Export CSV. A 400-word lab note is due before the next block.

Design constraints we are refusing to violate

What keeps a cross-listed 300-level lab honest

No install night

Arts students and Science students arrive with different laptops and unequal setup patience. If it does not run in a browser tab, it is homework for us, not for them.

One equation per night

The 3-hour block cannot also be a programming course and a statistics course. The code exists to expose one computation, then get out of the way.

Both faculties can remix

Every lab has an aesthetic fork and a factorial fork. Grading values a clear prediction and a visible change, not professional JavaScript.

Assessment sketch

Grade the argument, not the repo polish

40% weekly lab notes (phenomenon, prediction, slope or d′, one limitation). 20% two in-class critiques. 40% final pair project: a browser experiment or instrument that makes one computation legible to a classmate who missed the week it was taught.
