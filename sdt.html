From “how fast” to “how separable”

Sensitivity is not the same as saying yes.

Search slopes ask how long binding takes. Signal detection asks whether two internal states can be told apart at all. Arts students meet this as a question about discrimination in a noisy field. Science students meet it as the 300-level move past percent-correct.

d′ = z(Hit) − z(False alarm) Criterion c = −0.5 [z(Hit) + z(FA)] decides how willing you are to say “yes.”
