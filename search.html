Feature search vs conjunction search

Find the red T. Time how the field fights back.

Click the red T as soon as you see it. When every distractor is a green T, color should pop out and the slope should stay near zero. When red Ls and green Ts share the target’s features, the mind has to bind color to shape. That binding costs milliseconds per extra item.
