PSYCH 302 / 305 · Midterm: your project plan · 15%
Due Wednesday 28 October 2026, 21:00 (in class). Work on your own.

For the rest of the term you will work on a project you choose. It can be anything that uses code and has something to do with psychology or how people think and act. Big or small is up to you. You do not have to finish it: on 2 December you tell the class how far you got. More details: project page.

What to do:

  1. Write your plan. Open week10-project/CHECKIN.md in your course repository, answer the questions in it, and save it to GitHub (Commit, then Sync, in VS Code). Help: workflow page.
  2. Paste into this Canvas box: week10-project/CHECKIN.md, one sentence with your idea and your first step, and whether you used an AI tool (and if so what for).
  3. Talk it over. During class the instructor meets with each student for about 5 minutes. Bring your plan. You talk through your idea together and agree on a first step.

How it is marked (15 points):

  • Your idea · 3 points. Full marks: what you want to make or find out, and why, clear enough that someone else could explain it back. Some marks: vague or hard to follow.
  • How you will do it · 3 points. Full marks: what you will build or do, and what you will use (and any data). Some marks: only a general idea of how.
  • Your steps · 3 points. Full marks: a first step, what you hope to have by 2 December, and a bonus goal. Some marks: one or two of these.
  • What you have and what might go wrong · 3 points. Full marks: what you can start from, one thing that might go wrong, and a backup plan. Some marks: only part of this.
  • Chat with the instructor · 3 points. Full marks: you come to the 5-minute chat with your plan and leave with a first step you both agree on. No marks: no chat.
  • Use of AI: required, no points.

A missing part gets no marks. We mark how clear your plan is, not how big it is. Missed class for a reason the instructor accepts? Hand in the plan by Friday 30 October, 17:00, and email the instructor to set up your chat. Do not include classmates’ names or data.
