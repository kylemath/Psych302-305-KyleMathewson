PSYCH 302 / 305 · Introduction to Computational Methods in Psychology
University of Alberta · Fall 2026 · Wednesday 18:00–21:00

This course teaches computing tools used in psychological research: a GitHub repository, a browser editor, version control, a data file, and a written report. The examples are ordinary psychology. Simple Syllabus is the official outline (weights, late work, letter grades). Canvas is the gradebook and the assignments. The handbook is the laboratory site.

This week

  1. Wednesday 9 September is Week 2: p5.html (lesson), then copy rt.html into your private repo, personalize it, and collect a block. How you submit: workflow.html.
  2. Week 2 · Laboratory report — due Tuesday 15 September, 17:00. Paste the path to your note, your modified RT page, the CSV, prediction, n / mean / SD, one limitation, and the AI disclosure.
  3. If Week 0 is still incomplete, submit the GitHub form now and accept the private-repo invite. Do not buy Copilot.

Assessment

  • 50% weekly laboratory reports (weeks 1–9; drop the lowest)
  • 15% individual midterm check-in (28 October)
  • 35% individual final project (2 December): running page plus a short in-class presentation. This is not a registrar exam. Self as participant. A browser game that studies a question is allowed.

Marking: rubrics. Official late work and letter grades: Simple Syllabus (same for 302 and 305).
