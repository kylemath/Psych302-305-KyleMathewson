Procedure and data

How

One trial or one completion, in order. State the exclusion rule in milliseconds or item numbers.

Weak versus clear

The page must be runnable from the text

Weak: “I did the reaction-time task on the website.” Clear: “Each trial showed a fixation, then a gold square after 800–2200 ms. I pressed the space bar. Twenty scored trials. Times under 150 ms or over 2500 ms were stored and excluded from the mean.”
